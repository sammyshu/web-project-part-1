What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your application within two business days.
  2. Shortlisted applicants have a 20-minute phone conversation.
  3. Final interviews are held at our Doha office or online.
  4. All applicants receive an outcome, successful or not.

If you need adjustments at any stage of the process, or would like to discuss cultural safety during recruitment, email careers@matchfit.com.qa. We welcome applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people for every role we advertise.
