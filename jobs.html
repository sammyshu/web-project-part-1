Careers at MatchFit

Help us build the websites and apps that keep community sport moving.

Join our digital team

MatchFit runs club memberships, event registration and fitness tracking for community sports clubs, coaches and their members. Our digital team builds the tools they use every week, and we are hiring for two roles.

Applications for both roles close on 30 November 2026.

Current vacancies

Front-End Developer, Member Portal

Build and look after the pages our members use to join a club, book sessions and check their training progress.

Reference number
FE204
Salary
$78,000 to $90,000 a year plus super
Reports to
Digital Product Manager

Key responsibilities

  • Build responsive pages with HTML5 and CSS3.
  • Turn designs from the UX team into working pages.
  • Check pages work with screen readers and keyboards.
  • Fix bugs reported by clubs and members.
  • Keep code tidy and commented for the rest of the team.

Essential requirements

  • Diploma or degree in IT, or equal experience.
  • Solid knowledge of HTML5 and CSS3.
  • Understanding of web accessibility (WCAG).
  • Comfortable using Git and GitHub.

Preferable requirements

  • Experience with Jira or a similar tool.
  • Interest in sport or fitness.
  • Basic knowledge of web security.

UX/UI Designer, Event Registration

Design clear, simple screens that let people sign up for club events in a few taps, on any device.

Reference number
UX318
Salary
$72,000 to $85,000 a year plus super
Reports to
Head of Design

Key responsibilities

  • Sketch and prototype event sign-up flows.
  • Run short usability tests with coaches and members.
  • Create colour palettes and layouts that pass contrast checks.
  • Work closely with front-end developers.

Essential requirements

  • Portfolio showing web or app design work.
  • Experience with a design tool such as Figma.
  • Good understanding of accessible design.
  • Clear written and spoken communication.

Preferable requirements

  • Basic HTML and CSS skills.
  • Experience designing mobile first.
  • Volunteer or playing experience in community sport.

Working at MatchFit

Our team works a hybrid week, finishes early on club game days and gets a free gym membership. Both roles are based in Melbourne.

How to apply

  1. Pick the role that suits you.
  2. Note its five-character reference number.
  3. Fill in the form on the Apply page.
  4. We reply within two weeks.

Everyone is welcome
